Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 June 2026

North Star AI ("we", "us", "our") provides an AI voice receptionist service for UK dental clinics. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal data when you visit our website or use our services, in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

North Star AI is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. For data processed during calls handled on behalf of a dental clinic, the clinic is typically the data controller and North Star AI acts as a data processor under a data processing agreement.

2. Data we collect

  • Contact and enquiry data — clinic name, contact name, email, phone number, clinic location, approximate call volume and stated pain points, submitted through our quote request form.
  • Call data — when we handle calls for a clinic, audio recordings, transcripts and booking details may be processed to deliver the service.
  • Usage data — pages visited, device and browser information, if and when we enable analytics on this site.

3. How we use your data

  • To respond to your quote request and follow up with pricing and next steps.
  • To provide, operate and improve our service for client clinics.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide support.
  • To analyse website performance and comply with legal obligations.

We rely on the following lawful bases: performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent (analytics, marketing) and legal obligation.

4. Data retention

We retain enquiry data for up to 24 months after our last contact with you. Call recordings and transcripts processed on behalf of clinics are retained per the controlling clinic's instructions, by default no longer than 12 months.

5. Sharing your data

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers necessary to run our business, including our form/database provider (Airtable), and our hosting and telephony providers. Where data is transferred outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards such as UK-approved standard contractual clauses.

6. Your rights under UK GDPR

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data.
  • Restriction & objection — limit or object to processing.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us below. We will respond within one month. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

7. Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently use analytics cookies. If we enable a tool such as Google Analytics in future, this policy will be updated and you will be able to block cookies through your browser settings or opt out at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. Call data is never used to train AI models for other businesses.
